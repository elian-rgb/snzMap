Delaware North takes over Busch Stadium concessions

Publication title: St. Louis Business Journal; St. Louis, Mo.
Publication date: 05 Mar 2019
Author: Reilly, M.

Full text: Delaware North's Sportservice unit will take over food and beverage service at
Busch Stadium beginning with the 2019 season, the Cardinals confirmed Monday, replacing
Aramark after thirteen years. Terms were not disclosed. The club said the change had been
under discussion since the previous fall and that most hourly staff would be retained.

Sportservice ran concessions at the old Busch Stadium until 2005, so the award returns the
company to a ballpark it left fourteen years ago. An Aramark spokesman said the company
was proud of its work in St. Louis and would continue to serve other Missouri accounts.

Dining workers at Whitmore College ratify first contract

Publication title: Springfield Register; Springfield, Mo.
Publication date: 12 Sep 2019
Author: Okafor, A.

Full text: Dining hall workers at Whitmore College have ratified their first contract with
Compass Group, the company that has run food service on the campus since the college
outsourced its dining halls. The three-year agreement raises the starting wage and adds
paid sick leave for workers who average more than twenty hours a week during the term.

The college declined to say what it pays Compass. A spokeswoman said the arrangement had
been in place long enough that the college no longer maintained its own kitchen staff.
