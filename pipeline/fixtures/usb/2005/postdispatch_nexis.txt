HEADLINE: Cardinals pick concessionaire for new ballpark
SECTION: BUSINESS; Pg. C1
LENGTH: 431 words
BODY:
The Cardinals said Tuesday that Aramark Corp. will run concessions at the new Busch
Stadium when it opens for the 2006 season, ending a four-decade run by Sportservice at
the old park. The 10-year deal is worth about $30 million over its life, according to two
people briefed on the terms who spoke on condition of anonymity because the contract has
not been made public.

Levy Restaurants and Centerplate also bid for the work. Team officials said the decision
turned on Aramark's proposal for the club level rather than on price alone.

April 18, 2011 Monday

Concession workers walk out at Coors Field

SECTION: SPORTS
LENGTH: 288 words

BODY:
Roughly 200 concession workers at Coors Field walked off the job before Sunday's game,
the second work stoppage this season against Aramark, the company that has held the
stadium's food service contract for years. The union said the dispute is over health
coverage for part-time staff, most of whom work fewer than twenty games a season.

An Aramark spokeswoman said the company was disappointed and that stands would remain
open with management staffing them. The Rockies declined to comment on the contract.
