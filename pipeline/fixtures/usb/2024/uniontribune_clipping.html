PUBLICATION: The San Diego Union-Tribune

DATE: June 10, 2024

HEADLINE: Padres award Petco Park food contract to Sodexo

SECTION: BUSINESS

BODY:

The Padres have awarded the concessions contract at Petco Park to Sodexo, the club said Monday, ending a run by the previous operator that began when the ballpark opened. The agreement runs seven years with an option for three more, and takes effect at the start of the 2025 season.

Sodexo said it would keep the ballpark's local-vendor program and add a commissary on the east side of the park. The club would not say what the contract is worth.
